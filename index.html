Core Formulas Used

Displacement  Vd = N · π/4 · B² · S  ·  Mean piston speed  Cm = 2 · S · n
Speed-density airflow  ṁair = VE · ρman · Vd · n / (2 · 60)   with   ρman = MAP / (R · Tman)
Indicated pressure  IMEP = ηc · (LHV / AFR) · ρman · VE  ·  BMEP = IMEP − FMEP (Chen–Flynn style friction)
Torque  T = BMEP · Vd / (4π)  ·  Power  P = T · n / 5252 (hp / kW, lb·ft / N·m, rpm)

Theoretical Background & Academic Sources

The model implements the standard speed-density framework taught in engine fundamentals (Heywood, Internal Combustion Engine Fundamentals), with semi-empirical VE, friction and knock sub-models of the kind published in SAE and Elsevier journals.

Sub-models

Volumetric efficiency — Gaussian breathing curve around tuned rpm, scavenging gain with pressure ratio, port-flow choking at high rpm. Consistent with semi-empirical VE models in the literature (Kakaee & Mashadi, Proc. IMechE Part D, 2017).
Charge temperature (forced induction) — T₂ = T₁·(1 + (PR0.283 − 1)/ηc) for the compressor, then intercooler: T₃ = T₂ − ηic·(T₂ − Tamb).
Dynamic compression ratio — DCR computed from intake-valve-closing point (effective stroke), the classic Lanchester/DCR approach used for knock assessment.
Friction — FMEP = 30 + 8·(n/1000) + 1.5·(n/1000)² kPa (Chen–Flynn style, mean-piston-speed dependent).
Knock — Required-octane index from CR, boost, charge temp and enrichment; timing retarded 1.5°/RON of deficit, torque loss ≈ 1%/deg from MBT.
